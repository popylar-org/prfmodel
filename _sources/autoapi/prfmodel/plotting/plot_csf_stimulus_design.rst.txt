prfmodel.plotting.plot_csf_stimulus_design
==========================================

.. py:function:: prfmodel.plotting.plot_csf_stimulus_design(stimulus: prfmodel.stimuli.CSFStimulus, **kwargs) -> tuple[matplotlib.figure.Figure, matplotlib.axes.Axes]

   Plot the design of a contrast sensitivity function (CSF) stimulus.

   Plots the stimulus contrast over time with color indicating different spatial frequencies.

   :param stimulus: Stimulus object with contrast and spatial frequencies.
   :type stimulus: CSFStimulus
   :param \*\*kwargs: Keyword arguments passed to :func:`~matplotlib.pyplot.subplots`.

   :returns: * **fig** (*matplotlib.figure.Figure*) -- Figure object.
             * **ax** (*matplotlib.axes.Axes*) -- Axes object.

   .. seealso::

      :py:obj:`plot_csf_stimulus_curve`
          Contrast sensitivity curve on top of the stimulus design.
