prfmodel.plotting.plot_csf_stimulus_curve
=========================================

.. py:function:: prfmodel.plotting.plot_csf_stimulus_curve(stimulus: prfmodel.stimuli.CSFStimulus, csf: numpy.ndarray, **kwargs) -> tuple[matplotlib.figure.Figure, matplotlib.axes.Axes]

   Plot a contrast sensitivity curve over a stimulus design.

   Plots spatial frequency over contrast sensitivity (100 / contrast, with contrast in percent) and draws a contrast
   sensitivity curve on top. Blank frames, which have zero contrast or zero spatial frequency, are left out.

   :param stimulus: Stimulus object with contrast and spatial frequencies.
   :type stimulus: CSFStimulus
   :param csf: Contrast sensitivity curve. Must match the number of frames in ``stimulus``.
   :type csf: numpy.ndarray
   :param \*\*kwargs: Keyword arguments passed to :func:`~matplotlib.pyplot.subplots`.

   :returns: * **fig** (*matplotlib.figure.Figure*) -- Figure object.
             * **ax** (*matplotlib.axes.Axes*) -- Axes object.

   .. seealso::

      :py:obj:`plot_csf_stimulus_design`
          Stimulus contrast over time.
