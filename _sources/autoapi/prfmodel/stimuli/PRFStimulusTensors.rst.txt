prfmodel.stimuli.PRFStimulusTensors
===================================

.. py:class:: prfmodel.stimuli.PRFStimulusTensors



   Tensor-holding counterpart of a :class:`~prfmodel.stimuli.PRFStimulus`.

   Holds the population receptive field stimulus arrays as backend tensors. Should be created with
   :meth:`PRFStimulus.to_tensors`.

   :param design: The :attr:`PRFStimulus.design` array as a tensor.
   :type design: :data:`prfmodel.typing.Tensor`
   :param grid: The :attr:`PRFStimulus.grid` array as a tensor.
   :type grid: :data:`prfmodel.typing.Tensor`
