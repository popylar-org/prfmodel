prfmodel.stimuli.CFStimulusTensors
==================================

.. py:class:: prfmodel.stimuli.CFStimulusTensors



   Tensor-holding counterpart of a :class:`~prfmodel.stimuli.CFStimulus`.

   Holds the connective field stimulus arrays as backend tensors. Should be created with
   :meth:`CFStimulus.to_tensors`.

   :param distance_matrix: The :attr:`CFStimulus.distance_matrix` array as a tensor.
   :type distance_matrix: :data:`prfmodel.typing.Tensor`
   :param source_response: The :attr:`CFStimulus.source_response` array as a tensor.
   :type source_response: :data:`prfmodel.typing.Tensor`
