prfmodel.utils.calculate_eccentricity
=====================================

.. py:function:: prfmodel.utils.calculate_eccentricity(mu_x: numpy.ndarray | pandas.Series, mu_y: numpy.ndarray | pandas.Series) -> numpy.ndarray

   Calculate the eccentricity of two-dimensional population receptive field (pRF) centers.

   The eccentricity is the distance of the pRF center from the origin of the visual field (e.g., the center of the
   screen).

   :param mu_x: Horizontal coordinate of the pRF centers.
   :type mu_x: numpy.ndarray or pandas.Series
   :param mu_y: Vertical coordinate of the pRF centers.
   :type mu_y: numpy.ndarray or pandas.Series

   :returns: Eccentricity of the pRF centers in the units of `mu_x` and `mu_y`.
   :rtype: numpy.ndarray

   .. rubric:: Examples

   >>> import numpy as np
   >>> calculate_eccentricity(np.array([3.0, 0.0]), np.array([4.0, -2.0]))
   array([5., 2.])
